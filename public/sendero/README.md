# Sendero home assets

`moon.svg`, `landscape.svg`, and `intelligence.svg` are unchanged illustrations extracted from the user-supplied `Demo Sendero.html` reference (18 October 2026). They are used as external image assets on the administration home.

Montserrat (400 and 600) and Marcellus (400) are locally hosted Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included in `montserrat-OFL.txt` and `marcellus-OFL.txt`.

Styles are scoped to `.hoy-sendero`; other administration routes keep their existing visual system.
